# Huang seedfunding proposal

Narrated, animated overview of the Huang Complex Supercomputing Seed Fund proposal: mapping milk peptides to gut receptors.

**Live page:** https://kuhfeldrf.github.io/huang-seedfunding-proposal/

Eight scenes follow the seed fund narrative: digestion releases thousands of milk peptides whose functions are largely unexplored; why a receptor-centric screen beats one-peptide-at-a-time discovery; the ~3 million peptide–receptor pairs; Aim 1 (co-folding of receptor and peptide from sequence alone across 204 receptors, plus pharmacophore matching against endogenous peptide ligands at 83 receptors); validation at the μ-opioid receptor (8F7Q, Protenix + MSA); Aim 2 (activation MD: β-casomorphin opens TM6 so a G protein couples, swaps GDP for GTP and separates into Gα and Gβγ; casoxin binds but holds the receptor shut); the 144-GPU hero run; and the peptide–receptor map, which contrasts known and orphan receptors, groups receptors by shared peptides, and sends top candidates to lab validation (PDSP binding and cAMP assays). The network shown in the final scene is illustrative.

Narration is pre-recorded with the open-source Kokoro-82M neural text-to-speech model (British male voice `bm_george`, Apache-2.0) and stored as MP3s in `audio/`, so it sounds the same in every browser and needs no server. Captions follow along on screen.
