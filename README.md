# Huang seedfunding proposal

Narrated, animated overview of the Huang Complex Supercomputing Seed Fund proposal: mapping milk peptides to gut receptors.

**Live page:** https://kuhfeldrf.github.io/huang-seedfunding-proposal/

Nine scenes follow the seed fund narrative: digestion releases thousands of milk peptides whose functions are largely unexplored; why a receptor-centric screen beats one-peptide-at-a-time discovery; the ~3 million peptide–receptor pairs; Aim 1 (co-folding of receptor and peptide from sequence alone across 204 receptors, plus pharmacophore matching against endogenous peptide ligands at 83 receptors); validation at the μ-opioid receptor (8F7Q, Protenix + MSA); Aim 2 (activation MD: β-casomorphin switches the receptor on and a G protein carries the signal into the cell, while casoxin binds but holds it off); the 144-GPU hero run (about three years on an 8-GPU cluster vs weeks on the Huang Complex); the receptor activation map, with known vs orphan receptors, receptor grouping by shared peptides, lab validation of top peptides and new targets from the receptor map; and the project's outcomes and impact (infant nutrition, human health, Oregon economy, education and workforce). The network shown in scene 8 is illustrative.

Narration is pre-recorded with the open-source Kokoro-82M neural text-to-speech model (British male voice `bm_george`, Apache-2.0) and stored as MP3s in `audio/`, so it sounds the same in every browser and needs no server. Captions follow along on screen.
